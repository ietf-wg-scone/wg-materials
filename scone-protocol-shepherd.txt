# Document Shepherd Write-Up for draft-ietf-scone-protocol

*This template version is dated 4 July 2022.*

## Document History

1. Does the working group (WG) consensus represent the strong concurrence of a
   few individuals, with others being silent, or did it reach broad agreement?

The WG reached broad agreement.

2. Was there controversy about particular points, or were there decisions where
   the consensus was particularly rough?

The WG was unable to come to consensus in discussion around a first-packet
indication for SCONE support at IETF 123 in Madrid, requiring a chair proposal
to break the deadlock, quoted below from the minutes:

  Brian (chair hat): thinks there's a rough-ish consensus that it's probably
  useful to do something. We should take input from this discussion to turn into a
  PR that can be decided on at the next interim. We are going to have first client
  packet indication in SCONE and determine the deployment considerations; is that
  a deal breaker for anyone? (nobody indicated it was).

Subsequent work on the feature proceeded without further controversy.

3. Has anyone threatened an appeal or otherwise indicated extreme discontent? If
   so, please summarize the areas of conflict in separate email messages to the
   responsible Area Director. (It should be in a separate email because this
   questionnaire is publicly available.)

No.

4. For protocol documents, are there existing implementations of the contents of
   the document? Have a significant number of potential implementers indicated
   plans to implement? Are any existing implementations reported somewhere,
   either in the document itself (as [RFC 7942][3] recommends) or elsewhere
   (where)?

Beyond minutes of interop at the hackathon, there is no formal implementation
record maintained by the WG. Multi-vendor interop, including open source
implementations, has been demonstrated from the IETF 124 Montreal hackathon
forward; the protocol side is well-exercised, and hackathon results have moved
on to fine-tuning parameters as of IETF 126 Vienna.

## Additional Reviews

5. Do the contents of this document closely interact with technologies in other
   IETF working groups or external organizations, and would it therefore benefit
   from their review? Have those reviews occurred? If yes, describe which
   reviews took place.

The protocol is deeply intertwined with QUIC; the adopted protocol design was
proposed by a group of QUIC experts in response to an initial protocol proposal.

20. Describe the document shepherd's review of the IANA considerations section,
    especially with regard to its consistency with the body of the document.
    Confirm that all aspects of the document requiring IANA assignments are
    associated with the appropriate reservations in IANA registries. Confirm
    that any referenced IANA registries have been clearly identified. Confirm
    that each newly created IANA registry specifies its initial contents,
    allocations procedures, and a reasonable name (see [RFC 8126][11]).

The protocol requires two QUIC versions and one QUIC transport parameter; this
document requests both in its IANA considerations section. Though the
registrations list the QUIC WG, and not the SCONE WG, as the contact
point, this is appropriate in that SCONE is intended to close once its work is
complete, and QUIC is responsible for the continued maintenance of the protocol.
